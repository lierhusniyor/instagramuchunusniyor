# Cyberpunk video promptlari (Sora / Runway / Kling / Veo / Pika)

Hammasi vertikal 9:16, 5-10 soniya. Har promptga oxirida qo'shing:
`cinematic, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, volumetric fog, neon reflections on wet asphalt, film grain, 24fps, ultra detailed, no text`

1. **Ochilish (ilgak):** Low-angle tracking shot gliding forward down a rain-soaked cyberpunk megacity street at night, towering skyscrapers covered in pink and cyan holographic ads, flying drones with light trails, steam rising from manholes, giant retro sun glowing on the horizon.
2. **Qahramon:** Anime-style original heroine, short silver hair with glowing cyan streaks, transparent neon raincoat, cybernetic eye implant, turns to camera and smiles, slow dolly-in, city bokeh behind her, reflections in her visor. (Original character, no known IP.)
3. **Kuzatuv (oner):** One continuous crane shot rising from a neon noodle stall in a crowded alley up past layers of signs and cables to a rooftop skyline at dawn, flying cars crossing the frame, warm happy sunrise breaking through purple smog.
4. **Telefon yaqin plan:** Extreme macro close-up of a smartphone screen showing a social feed made of floating holograms, rain drops on glass, neon pink and cyan light sweeping across, rack focus to the city behind.
5. **Quvnoq finali:** The heroine jumps from a rooftop, time slows, confetti-like neon petals and holographic hearts burst around her, camera orbits 180 degrees, bright optimistic colors, golden-hour rim light.
6. **Robot do'st:** A small round friendly robot with a glowing smiley face follows the heroine through a night market, hovering, tilting its head, lanterns and holograms in the background, handheld camera, warm and playful mood.
7. **Metro:** Cyberpunk subway train arriving at an underground platform, windows flashing neon, wind blowing the heroine's coat, camera pans from the train lights to her happy face, tiled walls with holographic graffiti.
8. **Yomg'ir raqsi:** Heroine dancing in the rain on an empty neon street, puddles reflecting signs, slow-motion water splashes, orbiting camera, joyful energy, synthwave mood.
